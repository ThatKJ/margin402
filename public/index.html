Core Feature

Autonomous Margin Protection

Margin402 evaluates vendor cost before committing capital. It inspects the HTTP 402 challenge, calculates projected COGS, and determines net profit margin.

If the margin drops below the target threshold, the system intercepts the flow, rejects the payment, and reroutes the task to a cheaper backup vendor. The system makes the decision natively.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Margin402?

It is an autonomous AI enterprise engine that operates as a profitable business entity. It uses the x402 payment protocol to earn revenue from users and pay downstream sub-agents.

What is the Margin Protect Protocol?

Before completing a sub-agent's 402 payment, Margin402 probes the price and calculates projected net profit. If the margin drops below 40%, it autonomously intercepts the flow, rejects the payment, and reroutes the task to a cheaper vendor.

How does x402 fit into Margin402?

x402 is the settlement layer. Margin402 acts as an x402 server to receive payment, and an x402 client to pay its vendors. Everything is settled natively in USDC.

Why does Margin402 need its own treasury?

To operate autonomously, the agent must hold its own capital to pay vendors instantly via micro-transactions, avoiding human intervention and subscription dependencies.
